Payment

All fees can be paid by debit or credit card, direct bank deposit or cash. Fees are paid at the end of the appointment, or by direct debit before the session.

My fees reflect my training, experience and ability to work with a range of clients and issues, and my commitment to ongoing professional development so that I provide the best service for you. They remain considerably lower than the Australian Psychological Society's recommended fees of $390 for an initial 80-minute session and $300 for a standard 50-minute psychology session.

Secure, encrypted card information

Your appointment time is reserved just for you. To secure it I require a valid debit or credit card number (EFTPOS cards are not accepted). Your appointment is not fully booked until there is a debit or credit card on file. Your card number is stored in a secure card vault hosted by Halaxy, an encrypted practice management system.

Cancellation policy

Your card will not be charged until after your appointment, unless the cancellation policy is breached. Miguel typically has a full calendar and every appointment is important. A late cancellation or missed visit leaves a space in the day that could have been filled by another client. When an appointment is cancelled, the following fees apply:

Medicare rebates

You do not need a referral from a general practitioner to see a psychologist. However, a Mental Health Treatment Plan from a GP, psychiatrist or paediatrician is needed to claim the Medicare rebate for individual sessions of focused psychological counselling. Medicare subsidises 10 sessions per calendar year. Relationship and family therapy sessions are not eligible for the rebate.

To access a rebate, make an appointment with your GP and complete a Mental Health Care Plan. Tell the doctor's receptionist that you want a referral to a psychologist, as the GP needs about 30 minutes to discuss the plan with you. You must have the referral before your first appointment, because a GP cannot backdate a Mental Health Care Plan.

Areas of experience

A broad range of adult mental health concerns

  1. Clinical Psychology

    Counselling and psychotherapy for depression, anxiety, panic, trauma and chronic pain, using CBT, ACT, DBT, mindfulness and psychodynamic approaches.

  2. Forensic Psychology

    Psycho-legal reports, psychometric assessment and expert opinion for criminal, family law and personal injury matters across Victoria.

  3. Hypnotherapy

    Clinical hypnosis for anxiety, pain, anger, unwanted habits and addictions, on its own or alongside other therapy.

  4. Relationship Counselling

    Couples therapy, family therapy and individual relating therapy, including an intensive program for couples in crisis.

  5. Psychosexual Therapy

    Sex-positive therapy for individuals and couples of all orientations, working on desire, intimacy and sexual difficulties.

  6. Transpersonal Psychotherapy

    Psycho-spiritual healing and growth through meditation, breathwork, hypnosis, creative imagination and art as therapy.

  7. Psychotherapy for Trauma

    Treatment of unresolved trauma through emotional expression, self-compassion, mind-body awareness and narrative work.

  8. Adult ADHD

    Assessment with clinical interview and standardised questionnaires, and treatment with CBT and mindfulness-based therapy.

Why choose Miguel

Client-centred care from an experienced clinician

I am a practising psychologist and psychotherapist based in Box Hill, Melbourne. I also consult by videoconference (Zoom) or telephone wherever you are in Australia or the world, in English and Spanish.

I provide a range of specialised therapeutic services to support your mental, emotional and relational wellbeing. With over 25 years of experience, I offer individual counselling and psychotherapy, family therapy, relationship counselling, psychosexual therapy and transpersonal psychotherapy. My approach is client-centred, so the work is shaped by your needs and preferences, whether you want to resolve conflict, improve intimacy or explore spiritual growth.

Clinical approach

Regardless of the therapeutic framework, my approach always has you at the centre of the process. A non-judging, client-centred value is the foundation for every other approach.

Questions

Frequently asked

The questions people ask before a first appointment. If yours is not here, call or text 0438 227 314 or email miguel@miguelpsychologist.com.au.

Do you offer telehealth or online sessions?

Yes. You can access my services from anywhere in Australia and beyond, by telephone or by videoconference (Zoom). Telehealth suits people who cannot easily leave home, parents who find it hard to arrange care for their children, and anyone without a practitioner with the right expertise nearby. To reserve a telehealth consultation, contact me by email or phone.

Is therapy tailored to me?

Yes. My client-centred approach means therapy is shaped to your situation, so the work stays relevant to your goals and experiences.

Do you combine different approaches?

I integrate psychodynamic, cognitive-behavioural and transpersonal practices to address your concerns fully and support your overall wellbeing.

Are your methods evidence-based?

My practice is grounded in scientifically validated techniques and current research. Over many years, I have refined my therapeutic approach to support meaningful and sustainable psychological change.

What services do you offer?

Individual counselling and psychotherapy, relationship counselling, family therapy and individual hypnotherapy, as well as forensic and medico-legal assessment.

What qualifications does Miguel have?

Miguel holds a Bachelor of Behavioural Science with Honours and has completed extensive training in evidence-based therapies, including cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) and dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT). He has also completed diploma-level postgraduate training in relationship counselling and family therapy.

What age ranges do you work with?

Adults of all ages.

What types of issues do you specialise in?

In over 25 years of practice Miguel has gained knowledge and experience across a very broad range of mental health issues, couples concerns and family issues.

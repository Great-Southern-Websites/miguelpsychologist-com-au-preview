Relationship counselling works on four things at once: attachment security, intimacy, stability and satisfaction. Here is what that involves.

## Attachment security

**Understanding attachment styles.** Counselling helps couples identify their attachment styles (secure, anxious, avoidant or disorganised) and see how those patterns shape the dynamics of the relationship.

**Building secure attachment.** Counselling guides couples towards secure attachment behaviours by encouraging open communication, emotional responsiveness and trust-building exercises. This includes working through past traumas and insecurities that get in the way of emotional safety.

## Intimacy

**Improving communication.** Counselling develops skills such as active listening, empathy, and expressing emotions and needs openly. This builds emotional intimacy by creating a safe space for both partners to share what they think and feel.

**Physical and emotional connection.** A counsellor may suggest activities that promote closeness, such as date nights, shared hobbies or affectionate gestures, which strengthen the bond between partners.

## Stability

**Conflict resolution strategies.** Couples learn fair fighting rules, compromise and problem-solving, so that disagreements are handled constructively and the relationship stays stable.

**Building a strong foundation.** Counselling helps couples set realistic expectations, establish boundaries and create a shared vision for the future. That shared understanding improves the stability and long-term viability of the relationship.

## Satisfaction

**Identifying and addressing unmet needs.** Counselling helps couples find the areas where their needs are not being met and work together on solutions, which may mean renegotiating roles, responsibilities or expectations.

**Fostering appreciation and gratitude.** Expressing appreciation for each other's efforts and positive qualities lifts satisfaction. Regularly acknowledging each other's contributions strengthens the emotional connection.

By working on these four areas, relationship counselling gives couples the tools and insight to understand their dynamics, communicate better, resolve conflict, and build a stronger and more fulfilling relationship.

title: "Psychosocial treatment of depression"
description: "How I treat depression: cognitive behavioural therapy, interpersonal therapy, psychodynamic work, a support network and lifestyle changes together."
date: 2024-11-29 09:58
---
When it comes to treating depression, I focus on psychosocial methods, which address both a person's psychological state and their social environment.

One of the main approaches is cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT). In CBT we identify and change the negative thought patterns and behaviours that contribute to depression, so that you develop more positive ways of thinking and better coping strategies.

Another method I use is interpersonal therapy (IPT), which improves relationships and the effectiveness of communication. Through IPT we address the relationship issues that may be contributing to depression and work on building healthier, more supportive connections with others.

I also find value in psychodynamic therapy, which explores the underlying emotional conflicts and past experiences that may be influencing your current feelings and behaviour. A deeper understanding of your psychological experience lets you work through those issues and reduce their effect on your mental health.

In addition, help with building a support network of understanding friends, family and support groups gives you a sense of connection and reduces isolation.

Combining these psychosocial treatments with lifestyle changes, such as regular exercise, a balanced diet and adequate sleep, helps you manage depression more effectively.
